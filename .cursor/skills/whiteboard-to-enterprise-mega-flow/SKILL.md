# Whiteboard → Mega Flow

## Required
- Image of the board/sketch
- Transcript or facilitation notes (narration beats illegible handwriting)

## Deliverable
One mega flowchart (Mermaid subgraphs) plus inventory under `artifacts/diagrams/`. Omit uncited edges; list open questions.

## Do not
- Treat this as screen UI (`/ingest-figma-sketch` + mockup-to-component).
- Invent swimlanes, systems, or decisions not in the image or notes.
